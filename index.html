Bar 7: live sport, drinks and good nights in Upton upon Severn

Bar 7 is a friendly independent pub and sports bar at 57 Old Street, Upton upon Severn. Join us for live sport, cold drinks, music, pool and darts in a relaxed setting within easy reach of Great Malvern and the wider Malvern Hills.

A welcoming local sports bar

Settle in for the match on our screens, meet friends for a drink or make Bar 7 part of your night out in Upton. Our bar serves lager, ale, cider, spirits, shots and bottled favourites, with a warm atmosphere and friendly service. The room comes alive for major fixtures, live performances and entertainment, while quieter evenings are ideal for a casual catch-up.

There is plenty to do beyond watching sport. Challenge your friends to a game of pool or darts, enjoy music and DJ nights, or simply find a comfortable spot at the bar. Groups are welcome, whether you are planning after-work drinks, a birthday or an informal celebration. See photos of the venue and recent nights in our pub gallery, or check our live sport and events page before you visit.
